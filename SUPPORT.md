## Before Filing an Issue

1. Check the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) — your issue may already be fixed in an unreleased version.
2. Check [existing issues](https://github.com/revyrlabs/Ferqon-Firmware/issues) to avoid duplicates.
3. Run `ferqonfw doctor` to verify your environment.
4. Include: hardware platform, OS, Python version, PlatformIO version, and the exact command that failed.
